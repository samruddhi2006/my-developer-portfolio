Menu hover previews are currently DISABLED (see src/components/header/nav/index.tsx).

To enable them, drop your own 800x800 PNGs here with these exact names,
then uncomment the <Image> block in nav/index.tsx:

  landing.png   (Home)
  about.png     (About)
  skills.png    (Skills)
  projects.png  (Projects)
  blog.png      (Blogs — if you re-add the link)
  contact.png   (Contact)

The motion toggle sits at the menu's bottom-right — keep previews on the
left/center so the two never overlap.
